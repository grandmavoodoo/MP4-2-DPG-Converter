Getting it onto your DS

  1. Copy the .dpg file to your R4 microSD card (a /DPG or /MOONSHL2 folder is tidy, but anywhere works).
  2. Boot the card, open Moonshell, browse to the file, and play.
  3. If audio is silent or wrong speed on DPG0/PCM, re‑encode as DPG4 (MP2) — that's what Moonshell 2 expects.
